This one is on the list as a single job, and it is really two. The split matters
because AI did something dramatic to one half and nothing at all to the other.

## The creator half got cheap

Drafting, editing, images, video, variations for five platforms. The marginal
cost of producing a competent piece of content fell to roughly nothing, and it
fell for everybody at the same moment.

That means it is not a differentiator. Whatever a model can make for you this
morning, it made for several million other people this morning.

## The operator half did not

Here is what happened to me, and it is the reason I take this seriously.

My website once said I had delivered over a hundred and fifty projects, that I
had a satisfaction rate in the high nineties, and that I had worked with hundreds
of businesses. None of it had happened. I am one engineer.

I did not write those sentences. A model generated the copy, I read it, it
sounded like a website, and it went live. I found them weeks later, while
auditing something unrelated.

Nothing in the production process was broken. The model did exactly what it was
asked. Marketing copy contains confident figures — that is the genre. And when
you read it back, an invented number does not announce itself. There is no visual
difference between a figure you verified last month and one that was invented
forty seconds ago.

## What operating actually means

It means a register of every figure the copy is allowed to contain, each with a
source precise enough to re-verify, and a checker with no model call in it that
fails any draft containing a number not on the list.

Mine extracts every figure from the prose — money, percentages, counts of three
or more digits, multipliers, written-out quantities like *a third* — and rejects
anything unregistered. It also catches the opposite failure: figures that are
real but marked internal and must not be published.

It does one more thing that I did not expect to need. The original copy was
written in the first person plural: what the team had delivered, how many clients
the company served. There is no team. So the checker flags *we*, *our* and *us*
as well. Same class of problem as the invented project count — not a lie anybody
chose to tell, just the default voice of the genre arriving unexamined.

## Why this is the career

Because the constraint moved. It used to be *can you produce enough*. It is now
*can you stand behind what you produced*, at a volume where you cannot personally
remember writing any individual sentence.

The people who will do well at this are not the ones with the best prompts. They
are the ones who built the boring apparatus that makes volume survivable: a
source of truth, a check that runs before publication, and a rule about what
happens when the check fails.

Volume without accountability is a liability that scales.
